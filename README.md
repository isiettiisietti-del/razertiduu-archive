RazerTiduu — Surviv.io & Survev.io Player Archive
Overview

RazerTiduu is a German player known in the Surviv.io and Survev.io communities for high-kill gameplay, solo-squad performances, damage records, and competitive 1v1 ability.

His gameplay history spans multiple eras of the games. He played on both EU and NA servers, adapting to different competitive environments. His reputation was built through public matches, aggressive play, high-kill games, and later head-to-head duels against other recognized players.

Official YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/@razertiduu1876
Competitive legacy

RazerTiduu’s playstyle combined aggressive public-match farming with the ability to compete in direct 1v1 encounters. His achievements cover several formats, including solo, solo duo, solo squad, Cobalt, 50v50, and team gameplay.

According to community accounts, his strongest 1v1 period across Surviv.io and Survev.io was between 2024 and 2026. During part of this period, he was regarded by some players as the leading 1v1 competitor. Names associated with his competitive scene and rivalries include Instinct, Hex, Saadzz, and Tenno.

These rankings reflect reported community opinion and should not be treated as an independently verified, official ranking.
